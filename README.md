# Girl, Boy, Bakla, Tomboy

## Introduction: 

This movie was about the quadruplets who were split into pairs and raised apart by their parents. They would then meet as adults by accident and will face a tough dilemma where one of them needs a liver transplant. One pair consist of a straight boy and girl, and the other are gay and lesbian respectively. Basically, this is a comedy film made in 2013 where one actor impressively played as all 4 quadruplets by himself.

## Synopsis: 

2 girls 2 boys are born into this world, the grandma took the pair of sibs (girl, boy) along with the father and left to America, leaving the other pair (bakla, tomboy) in the care of the mother. years later, the boy is sick and _needs a liver transplant_ from one of the quadruplets, and also the father is about to get an arranged marriage with his fiancée so he also has to get the mother to _sign the divorce papers_ too. The quadruplets then reunite by accident and some silly shenanigans happen among them. After some quarrels and challenges, eventually, the boy _got his transplant from the bakla's liver_, and the _divorce papers were signed_, causing the straight pair to return to America begrudgingly. However, seeing how the fiancée treats the children and grandma harshly, he _cancelled the marriage and broke up with her_, ending the situation with pure relief. With a very scuffed funeral exclusively planned by the bakla to buy time and tension, the father and his kids eventually managed to _return and reunite with the rest of the family_, bringing back the quadruplets, and the entire family as a happy one in the end
